Gluten Free Pizza

In the world of gluten free, you cannot be so sure that the items at the resteraunt are actually gluten free. With this easy to make Gluten Free Pizza recipie, yuu are in control. There are very few steps to thsi delicious pizza dough recipie and you can then add whatever toppings you like. Make this dough and let your creative mind fly. I like to use a sheetpan for this and make 1 large pizza for the whole family.

The directions below make 1 sheetpan sized pizza.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/3 Cups gluten-free pizza flowr (King Arthurs)
  • 1 1/4 Cups water, warm
  • 1 Tablespoon Sugar
  • 2 Teaspoons instant yeast
  • 3/4 Teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Beat ingredients together until dough is sticky but smooth. Scrap sides of bowl
  2. Cover in a seperate bowl and let rise for about an hour.
  3. Position a pizza stone or steel on the bottom rack of your oven and set oven to 500 degrees F.
  4. Pull Pizza stone or steel from oven and work dough onto the item of choice.
  5. Add all ingredients wanted on the pizza and bake for 18 minutes
  6. Pull pizza and let stand for 5-10 minutes to cool.
  7. Cut and enjoy.
